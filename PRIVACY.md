# Tidyset Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026

Tidyset processes datasets locally on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share dataset contents, filenames, cleaning recipes, analytics, advertising identifiers, or diagnostics.

Files are accessed only when you select them and exports are written only to locations you choose. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple; the developer does not receive your payment-card details. Tidyset does not use tracking technologies or third-party advertising SDKs.

You can remove local app data by deleting the app. Questions may be submitted through the repository’s Support page.
